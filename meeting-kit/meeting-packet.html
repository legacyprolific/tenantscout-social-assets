What it is

TenantScout is an HBU engine for commercial listings. From an offering memorandum it ranks the highest and best uses of the building (legal, physical, conversion cost and demand), explains the reasoning, and builds a call list of the businesses that fit each use.

What it changes for the firm

Consistent analysis

Every broker, every office, the same screening on day one.

Stronger pitches

Brokers walk into the listing presentation with the answer and the names behind it.

Faster leasing

Conversations start the day the listing signs.

More double-ends

The firm's broker finds the tenant for the firm's listing.

Value owners see

A plan with names attached, and work the owner can watch happen.

A record you keep

Leads, call outcomes and follow-ups live in a shared CRM.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't replace the broker's judgment. Every ranking comes with its reasoning, and any broker can override it.

On your data

Uploaded OMs are used to produce your outputs, processed by named service providers under contract. We do not sell personal information.

How it works

From the OM to the first call.

01
Upload

The OM or flyer. Text, photos, floor and site plans are all read.

02
Screen

Every use tested for zoning, physical fit, conversion cost and demand.

03
Rank

Top three uses with fit score, conviction and reasoning. Override any of them.

04
Call

The businesses that fit each use, with a reason to call each one.

05
Follow up

Leads seed the CRM, tasks come from call outcomes, renewal radar watches the tenants you place.

The engine, in plain words

  • ● A use the zoning prohibits never makes the list, so it never reaches a phone call.
  • ● Hard physical requirements knock a use out before it's scored.
  • ● Ready-to-occupy uses rank ahead of ones that need a big build-out.
  • ● Size fit and submarket demand decide the order.
  • ● Investment listings branch to buyer matching instead.

Your judgment drives it

Read the reasoning behind every ranked use. When you know the building better, override it, and the call list rebuilds around your call.

The engine does the screening. You make the call.
